## Buyer and staff journey

1. **Pick a package.** Express begins from a published design. Professional can proceed online after fit review. Bespoke Growth uses a prepared quote. An incomplete Express link returns to the catalog.
2. **Create or verify an account.** The selected package, plan, and period survive sign-in or email confirmation. Quotes are bound to the prepared email and the verified buyer. A different signed-in account can switch accounts and return to the quote.
3. **Review and pay.** The order shows the build, the recurring plan, the amount due before tax, and the renewal cadence. The buyer can open the service agreement before accepting it. Repeated submissions of the same checkout attempt reuse its order, saved selections, and Stripe idempotency key. The customer can resume an already-open payment from Purchases. A staff cancellation expires the open provider session before closing the order.
4. **Confirm and provision.** The Stripe webhook and the authenticated return page share an idempotent paid transition. The recorded Stripe session must match the order. The existing database allocator grants the exact buyer owner membership and creates the project and website. The existing atomic subscription function prevents concurrent provider updates from creating duplicate subscription rows. If a paid Stripe subscription cannot be read, provisioning retries.
5. **Open the project.** The confirmation screen gives the buyer one primary action when ready. The buyer can return from Purchases or from the account-owned welcome email. The setup link selects the purchased organization and exact project. While payment or setup is processing, the buyer can check again or return to the order later.
6. **Notify Vigil and start delivery.** A purchase queues a separate staff email job with retries, a per-order key, and a direct link to the admin order. Express also queues its purchased template for repository setup. Professional and Growth await the agreed review and scope steps. Intake submission still notifies staff, with the existing reconciliation pass for missed intake notices.
