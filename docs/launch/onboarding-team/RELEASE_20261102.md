Prepared on 2 November 2026 from production `6c64a0372e0ea556a6b531d32480302c5a8a2458`.

## Result

- Content Hub uses the approved dashboard typography, neutral vertical border/background fades, icon-led collections, readable controls and clear draft/publish states. The responsive product editor separates details and media, shows one image at a time with selectable/reorderable thumbnails, reuses Files and keeps its save controls visible. Team and the other collection editors share the same layout.

Onboarding products/services/menu retain all assigned private images and stable identities; Files keeps storage ownership, and Hub keeps item associations. Express with Care or higher receives the explicitly approved catalogue/Team access. Basic, tenant, role and prelaunch restrictions remain enforced.

Current production domain code, dependencies/security fixes and prelaunch overview/Website Preview work are retained. Virtue architecture/runtime work is outside this release.
