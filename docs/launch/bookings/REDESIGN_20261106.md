# Booking workspace redesign

The original appointment workflow worked, but its fixed 24-hour calendar and stacked setup forms made frequent use cumbersome. This revision gives Calendar, Requests, History and Setup focused workspaces using the dashboard’s surfaces, typography and royal blue accents.

## Client experience

- Calendar fits the remaining desktop viewport. Its time scale includes the selected people’s working hours and any appointments outside those hours. Month uses six bounded rows; agenda and appointment details scroll within their panels. Smaller screens open appointment details in a dismissible dialog.
- Requests and History use selectable rows beside a single detail panel. Existing approval, cancellation, rescheduling, attendance and inquiry actions remain available. History includes past appointments, terminal outcomes and expired requests, rather than every future appointment.
- Setup has direct sections for Booking rules, People & hours, Services, Time off, Website widget and Connections. Phone users select a section from a compact menu. Forms stay mounted when switching sections or people/services so unfinished values remain available. Save each form before navigating away from Setup.
- Weekly availability uses compact day rows with separate windows. Adding another window starts after the last window rather than inserting an overlapping default.
- Google Calendar has its own compact Connections section. Eligible owners/managers see the connection button when configured. Staff impersonation explains that the business owner must connect their own account. Connection scope and one-way syncing are unchanged.

## Website experience

A compatible site can show inline booking or a button opening an accessible native dialog. Customers choose a service and date, then an available time, then provide their details. Owners can choose a monthly calendar or date input, optional person/space filter and label, heading, introduction, button text and one of three readable accent colors. Setup has an interactive, fictional customer preview.

Each service continues to reserve its configured person/space. Selecting “All options” shows services across resources; it does not promise automatic assignment, restaurant tables or group capacity. Restaurant reservations are a separate phase, explicitly chosen by the owner.

The public widget still reads authoritative availability and submits through the existing CAPTCHA, registered-origin, rate-limit, idempotency and guest-management boundaries. Management credentials stay in URL fragments. The public configuration exposes resource names only when the owner enables that selector, and includes the business date and booking horizon for the date picker. No communications, invitations or Google connections are activated by presentation settings.

## Schema and rollout

Migration `20261105000064_booking_experience.sql` adds a validated JSON presentation field and a manager-only, Care-entitled save RPC with an audit event. The public configuration projection remains server-only and subject to existing website eligibility/origin checks. Defaults remain inline, with no resource names published. Existing activation and component-verification gates remain in place.

Apply this migration to an isolated preview database before testing persisted settings there, and to production only during the reviewed rollout with a fresh backup. Generated migration-64 types were merged while retaining legacy production definitions. Rebuilt Design Engine and Express booking assets ship together. No production schema, client activation or connection changes are part of this review branch. Scar remains contact-only.

- Type checking, production build and the migration/RLS/concurrency validation passed. Migration validation includes widget persistence without activation, invalid-setting rejection, member/anonymous/cross-tenant denial, public resource-name privacy and the booking horizon.
- Browser review: 1366 × 768 laptop, 1710 × 1017 desktop, 390 × 844 phone, light/dark themes; Calendar day/week/month, Requests/History, mobile appointment dialogs, setup navigation and owner/staff connection states. Laptop and phone calendar checks both had `main.scrollHeight === main.clientHeight`.
- In-progress availability names survive switching to Services and back. Popup person filtering changes the service, date/time selection carries to the details step, focus moves to the name field, fictional submission produces “Preview only,” and Escape returns focus to the launch button.
