# Experimental shortlist · awaiting human selection

These five concepts collectively cover useful evidence capabilities. They are not approvals or Production registrations.

## E01 · A voice, in the margin

A strong single voice without requiring a testimonial collection.

- Content requirements: testimony {quote, author, portrait?, provenance} + annotation; source, attribution, timeframe, context and publication permission before client use.
- Implementation: Low · quote, attribution, provenance and optional portrait.
- Responsive: Medium · preserve identity/quote relationship.
- Interaction: Low · native source disclosure.
- Future page potential: Home, About, Testimonials lead.


## E02 · The outcome equation

Reusable result narrative with credible baseline and calculation.

- Content requirements: result {before, after, unit, direction, basis, provenance} + explanation + voice; source, attribution, timeframe, context and publication permission before client use.
- Implementation: Medium · numeric formatting and method validation.
- Responsive: Medium · paired values and long units.
- Interaction: Low · disclosure only.
- Future page potential: Results, service outcomes, customer case lead.


## E03 · The change dossier

Serious transformation that explains what changed and what was done.

- Content requirements: subject + baseline {title, body, media?} + intervention {title, body, duration} + outcome {title, body, media?} + result + voice + limitation; source, attribution, timeframe, context and publication permission before client use.
- Implementation: Medium · stage relationships, comparable media and provenance.
- Responsive: High · chapter hierarchy must survive narrow widths.
- Interaction: Low · all proof remains exposed.
- Future page potential: Results, Customer Stories, transformation feature.


## E04 · Case cross-section

Connects service claims to concrete case evidence and future detail destinations.

- Content requirements: case {client, challenge, intervention, result, voice, media?, destination {kind,key,label}}; source, attribution, timeframe, context and publication permission before client use.
- Implementation: Medium · case record plus host destination adapter.
- Responsive: Medium · caption reflows out of media.
- Interaction: Low · host link in production, local synopsis in Lab.
- Future page potential: Work index, Case Studies lead, service evidence.


## E05 · The credential library

Covers awards, certification, press and partners with accountable scope.

- Content requirements: 3–12 records {organization, kind, title, scope, year, expires?, provenance}; source, attribution, timeframe, context and publication permission before client use.
- Implementation: Medium · validity, permission and source contracts.
- Responsive: Medium · index-to-record relationship.
- Interaction: Medium · selected record plus all-record fallback.
- Future page potential: About, Recognition, Trust center introduction.

E07 is the next useful specialist proof when a real review sample and selection policy exist. E12 is valuable once multiple substantiated cases justify a discovery surface. Neither is excluded from review; the five-item shortlist prioritizes capability breadth and evidence feasibility.
