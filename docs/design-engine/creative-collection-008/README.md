## Evidence integrity

Study-only strict schemas live in `design-engine/preview/collection-008/contracts.ts`. Each evidence record declares a source, attribution, timeframe and context. The provenance union distinguishes **demo**, **client-supplied**, and **verified**. Non-demo records require a publication-authorization declaration and a record reference; verified records additionally require an HTTP(S) source and a dated verifier/method. This is a future contract proposal, not independent verification or a new production gate. `evidencePublicationIssues` identifies demo records and invalid payloads.

All fixture evidence remains visibly fictional. Reviews derive sample counts, average and distribution from the supplied records; critical reviews remain included. Verified-purchase claims require a verified evidence record. No real awards, client endorsements, brand marks, ratings, review counts or outcomes are asserted. Before/after worksheets visualize synthetic values and never establish a real transformation.

## Per-study completion report

### E01 · A voice, in the margin

One attributed statement occupies a reading field; a tall portrait and an editorial annotation establish who spoke and why this experience matters.

- **Family:** Direct customer voice
- **Content/evidence contract:** testimony {quote, author, portrait?, provenance} + annotation. Evidence records carry the provenance union described above.
- **Scale:** Minimal · exactly one voice.
- **Media:** One optional portrait, with consent and honest relationship to the speaker. Demo portrait is illustrative, not the quoted person.
- **Capability:** section-oriented.
- **Responsive:** Portrait becomes a narrow identity column beside attribution; quotation follows at a bounded reading size. Source remains adjacent.
- **Interaction:** Native source disclosure; no sequencing or hidden quote.
- **Motion:** Propose existing MediaReveal on the portrait only; quote remains still. Current motion none.
- **Accessibility:** blockquote/figcaption attribution, labelled portrait, native details, readable full quotation.
- **Composition:** Quiet proof after Full Scene or a dense Services section; avoid two oversized quotations in a row.
- **Limits:** A weak or anonymous quote cannot support the same hierarchy; no rating or aggregate count.
- **Adaptation layers:** security editorial/publication; strength humanist/salon; furniture fashion/runway.

### E02 · The outcome equation

Baseline and outcome share a typographic equation. A proportional comparison, explicit arithmetic and measurement basis explain the number before a supporting voice signs it.

- **Family:** Quantitative result + voice
- **Content/evidence contract:** result {before, after, unit, direction, basis, provenance} + explanation + voice. Evidence records carry the provenance union described above.
- **Scale:** Minimal · one measured comparison and one quote.
- **Media:** None required. Numbers and measurement context are the evidence.
- **Capability:** section-oriented.
- **Responsive:** Before and after stay paired horizontally; the equation wraps its explanatory unit below. Measurement notes occupy full width.
- **Interaction:** Open measurement source and inspect arithmetic. No animated counter.
- **Motion:** Propose restrained FadeReveal of the complete equation, never count from zero. Current none.
- **Accessibility:** Text labels and values accompany bars; before/after meaning does not depend on color, width or arrow.
- **Composition:** Balances image-heavy HX02 or Work. Avoid immediately adjoining other oversized number sections.
- **Limits:** Comparable units and measurement basis required; before zero yields no relative percentage. Correlation is not causation.
- **Adaptation layers:** security technical/precision; strength poster/billboard; furniture geometric/gallery.

### E03 · The change dossier

Three unequal chapters preserve the original condition, the intervention and the observed outcome. Paired media supports a process record; limitations qualify the apparent change.

- **Family:** Transformation
- **Content/evidence contract:** subject + baseline {title, body, media?} + intervention {title, body, duration} + outcome {title, body, media?} + result + voice + limitation. Evidence records carry the provenance union described above.
- **Scale:** One transformation · exactly three stages.
- **Media:** Optional baseline/outcome records. Matching subject and capture conditions are a publication responsibility; diagrams can be more honest than unrelated photographs.
- **Capability:** section-and-page-capable.
- **Responsive:** A numbered case spine anchors three different chapter forms: compact baseline, inset intervention, expanded outcome. No image drag interface.
- **Interaction:** All stages visible; source disclosure supplements the reading sequence.
- **Motion:** Propose existing MediaReveal per complete chapter. No scroll pinning or Motion Engine extension.
- **Accessibility:** Ordered stages, h3 labels, textual state descriptions, limitations visible before source details.
- **Composition:** Works under H12 when the Hero compares a different subject; otherwise repeated comparison competes. Gives Commerce a customer-outcome bridge.
- **Limits:** Never infer outcome from a generated image; real transformation needs comparable media, dates and measurement conditions.
- **Adaptation layers:** security neo-grotesk/publication; strength humanist/salon; furniture luxury/gallery.

### E05 · The credential library

Recognition is catalogued as inspectable records: issuer, scope, type, year and validity. A selected accession sits beside a typographic index, without invented seals or logos.

- **Family:** Trust / recognition
- **Content/evidence contract:** 3–12 records {organization, kind, title, scope, year, expires?, provenance}. Evidence records carry the provenance union described above.
- **Scale:** Moderate / large · 3–12 recognitions.
- **Media:** No logos required; supplied official marks could be reviewed later with permission. Current study uses text records.

### E06 · People, in their own words

A slow typographic loop presents attributed voices, with a complete unequal reading wall available below or in Still mode. Credibility comes from distinct experiences, not a summed rating.

- **Family:** Human evidence / chorus
- **Content/evidence contract:** 4–9 voices {quote, author, portrait?, provenance}. Evidence records carry the provenance union described above.
- **Scale:** Moderate · 4–9 distinct voices.
- **Media:** Optional documentary portraits on selected voices; not every quote receives an avatar.
- **Capability:** section-and-page-capable.
- **Responsive:** The loop clips inside its own viewport without widening the page. Still mode becomes a continuous reading wall; images remain with their attributed voice.
- **Interaction:** Slow looping attributed voices with Pause/Resume and hover pause; opening the complete reading wall pauses the loop. Still mode exposes every voice and source.
- **Motion:** Implemented: existing Marquee, slow continuous loop; pauses offscreen, in a hidden tab, on hover, on explicit pause and while reading sources. Reduced motion and Still use the complete static chorus.
- **Accessibility:** Independent figures and blockquotes; DOM order matches reading order; each source owns its attribution.
- **Composition:** A human pause after technical service evidence; do not combine directly with another quote-dominant study.
- **Limits:** Not suitable for long transcripts or thousands of reviews; curator must avoid implying representativeness.
- **Adaptation layers:** security playful/salon; strength brutalist/billboard; furniture humanist/publication.

### E07 · The review reading room

An honest sample distribution introduces a readable review journal. Source filters expose different perspectives; lower ratings remain present and the sample never masquerades as platform-wide sentiment.

- **Family:** Reviews / distribution
- **Content/evidence contract:** 3–30 reviews {rating, quote, author, date, platform, verifiedPurchase, provenance} + selectionPolicy. Evidence records carry the provenance union described above.
- **Scale:** Moderate / large · 3–30 supplied reviews.
- **Media:** None. Rating counts derive only from supplied records.
- **Capability:** page-capable.
- **Responsive:** Compact average and five labelled distribution rows precede a full-width journal. Source controls wrap; review text never truncates.
- **Interaction:** Source filtering; sample average/distribution stay explicitly whole-sample, visible-review count changes. All reviews button resets.
- **Motion:** None proposed; stable reading position takes priority over animated ratings.
- **Accessibility:** Accessible textual rating out of five, dates, labelled bars, polite count, visible non-verified status. No star-only meaning.
- **Composition:** Works after customer transformation and before product collection; add breathing room after dense Services.
- **Limits:** No ingestion, platform totals, independent verification or fake verified badges. Sample must be described.
- **Adaptation layers:** security humanist/publication; strength playful/salon; furniture technical/precision.

### E08 · In conversation

A cinematic still introduces a named speaker, then a question-and-answer transcript exposes specific experience. The transcript is the core evidence, with optional genuine video.

- **Family:** Interview / video testimony
- **Content/evidence contract:** speaker + provenance + still? + video? + 2–6 question/answer exchanges. Evidence records carry the provenance union described above.
- **Scale:** Minimal · one interview.
- **Media:** Optional interview still or user-started video using existing caption/transcript schema. Demo has no playable video and labels the still as illustrative.
- **Capability:** section-and-page-capable.
- **Responsive:** Still becomes a shallow establishing frame; speaker precedes a transcript with questions as margin-free headers.
- **Interaction:** Native transcript chapter disclosures; first answer open, full transcript also available. Optional native video controls only with a real source.
- **Motion:** Propose MediaReveal on still; never autoplay. New timed transcript synchronization is deferred.
- **Accessibility:** Semantic Q&A headings, native summaries, full transcript fallback; speech video requires captions, no false play button.
- **Composition:** Good after dense quantitative evidence; avoid a second cinematic section immediately under a photographic Hero without a text interval.
- **Limits:** No video host integration, modal player or timed chapters; still is not proof that an interview occurred.
- **Adaptation layers:** security fashion/runway; strength editorial/publication; furniture geometric/gallery.

### E10 · Progress, with a paper trail

Successive dated observations form a rising or falling typographic staircase. Every measurement is paired with the event and method that make change interpretable.

- **Family:** Longitudinal results
- **Content/evidence contract:** label + unit + basis + 3–6 points {date,value,event,provenance}, strictly increasing dates. Evidence records carry the provenance union described above.
- **Scale:** One metric · 3–6 observations.
- **Media:** None; events and method carry the narrative.
- **Capability:** section-oriented.
- **Responsive:** Horizontal progression becomes a dated vertical ledger; values retain comparable type size. No chart panning.
- **Interaction:** All points visible, each source expandable.
- **Motion:** Propose StaggerReveal of whole observations. No counter tween and no novel motion dependency.
- **Accessibility:** Ordered dates, explicit units at each observation, source and method text. Position never substitutes for numeric meaning.
- **Composition:** Useful between an open Story and image-led Work; unlike E02 it shows sustained observation rather than one endpoint claim.
- **Limits:** Consistent measurement basis required; missing intervals and intervention causality must be disclosed by the host.
- **Adaptation layers:** security brutalist/billboard; strength technical/precision; furniture poster/publication.

## Adaptation findings

Every study has three validated/rendered adaptations, not simply a color switch. BOUNDARY / PRACTICE uses synthetic triage time (48 → 18 minutes), ownership worksheets, a larger relationship register and technical context. EVERYDAY STRONG uses attendance (9 → 21 of 24 sessions), community voices, an illustrative training environment and no physiological transformation claim. STILL / FORM uses reuse inventory (8 → 28 of 32 chairs), workshop decisions, architectural/material imagery and a smaller story collection. Type and art direction change independently across the three; defaults span ten type profiles.

Evidence counts deliberately differ: credentials 5/3/7; voices 6/8/4; reviews 6/9/4; relationships 8/5/6; customer stories 3/4/2. Minimal studies remain minimal rather than accepting an arbitrary universal payload. Missing-media and long-copy cases preserve the text evidence and source trail.

## Engine limitations discovered

The production catalog has no evidence-specific provenance/publication pipeline; the new contracts remain local proposals. A declared verified status cannot authenticate a source or consent. Host-owned semantic destinations still need a future route resolver. Real review ingestion, logo licensing, portrait consent, redaction, video delivery and source retention are absent. Video framing uses the existing optional video schema; the fixtures deliberately contain no invented playable interview. Timed transcript synchronization and new metric choreography remain future Motion requirements.

Production typography overrides are not universally available on older Navigation: the preview wrapper respects the actual declared override keys, rather than imposing a uniform override. A malformed generated `.next/dev/types/validator.ts` blocked the initial typecheck; preserved that disposable file outside the repository and regenerated route types. No production source fix was needed for it.

No production promotion, Collection 009, finished case/testimonial/results pages, page recipes, automatic composition, external review integration, dedicated Motion expansion or Scar changes. Stop here for human creative review.
