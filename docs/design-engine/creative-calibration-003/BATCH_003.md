# Creative Batch 003 — twelve review concepts

30 September 2026. **Concept stage only.** Review in `/admin/design-lab` → **Batch 003 concepts**. There are no new production components or registry entries. ## H17 — The Front Page

A cover story gives the brand an editorial point of view.

- **Mechanism:** An oversized publication masthead anchors an unequal story spread; headline, scene and marginal abstract form one reading path.
- **Predictable answer rejected:** A large serif headline above a centered image. Replace it with a masthead and a real story hierarchy.
- **Direction:** editorial typography / publication art direction.
- **Motion and interaction proposal:** Still first frame; one optional image wipe on entry. No word stagger.
- **Mobile:** Masthead, headline, image, abstract; side folio becomes a byline.
- **Three industry translations:** Architecture journal: place story; Food producer: harvest edition; Research organization: field report.

## H18 — Open Circuit

Show the system working before describing it.

- **Mechanism:** A single legible signal plot is the visual anchor; a readout and proposition share the plot's baseline. Data must be real or explicitly illustrative.
- **Predictable answer rejected:** SaaS dashboard cards and decorative grids. Use one meaningful signal and a clear claim instead.
- **Direction:** technical typography / precision art direction.
- **Motion and interaction proposal:** User-selectable time window in a later prototype; no fake live feed. Static chart in this study.
- **Mobile:** Headline then signal, with readout underneath. Do not squeeze axis labels.
- **Three industry translations:** Energy: output signal; Audio studio: frequency response; Logistics: route continuity.

## H19 — The Bill

An event identity with the urgency of a physical bill.

- **Mechanism:** A vertical date ticket interrupts a stacked title; the action sits on a full-width program strip. The date is content, not decoration.
- **Predictable answer rejected:** Big text over a concert photograph. Build a type-led event bill with ticket and running order.
- **Direction:** poster typography / billboard art direction.
- **Motion and interaction proposal:** Short vertical program-strip reveal; display text stays still. Study is static.
- **Mobile:** Title and date remain side-by-side; program strip wraps into a list.
- **Three industry translations:** Music venue: season opening; Museum: late exhibition; Fitness: regional competition.

## H20 — A Place at the Table

An invitation shaped around a person's reason for visiting.

- **Mechanism:** A friendly question opens into a broad subject image, with a conversational route sentence below. Choices have useful destinations rather than becoming cards.
- **Predictable answer rejected:** Wellness headline with three floating cards. Use one invitation and an image that establishes belonging.
- **Direction:** humanist typography / salon art direction.
- **Motion and interaction proposal:** No entrance motion; later route selection may update the contextual sentence.
- **Mobile:** Question, broad image, route sentence. Choice links wrap with generous touch spacing.
- **Three industry translations:** Community venue: gather or learn; Hospitality: stay or dine; Furniture: home or workspace.

## H21 — Index / Now

A practice defined by what it is doing now.

- **Mechanism:** The proposition is part of a large live-work index; one open row becomes a panorama, with the next row visible as continuation.
- **Predictable answer rejected:** Portfolio thumbnails in a grid. Make the selected project occupy the row's entire visual field.
- **Direction:** neo-grotesk typography / precision art direction.
- **Motion and interaction proposal:** A later keyboard-operable disclosure resizes only the selected row; no hover-only imagery.
- **Mobile:** Keep the thesis first; stack rows and let selected media expand vertically.
- **Three industry translations:** Architecture: current civic work; Film company: current releases; Environmental group: active sites.

## H22 — Between Acts

The pause between images becomes an expressive part of the identity.

- **Mechanism:** Two oversized italic phrases bookend a low photographic ribbon; a small side caption creates an intentional interruption in the reading rhythm.
- **Predictable answer rejected:** Fashion split screen with a portrait. Use a horizontal interval and typography that passes through the scene's edges.
- **Direction:** fashion typography / runway art direction.
- **Motion and interaction proposal:** Slow horizontal media reveal only; no perpetual parallax or animated type.
- **Mobile:** Phrases become top and bottom bookends around a taller image ribbon.
- **Three industry translations:** Dance company: seasonal program; Fashion atelier: new collection; Ceramic studio: firing story.

## H23 — Edition One

An edition has provenance before it has a sales pitch.

- **Mechanism:** An oversized edition numeral and a narrow product portrait create a single central lockup; a precise caption and quiet side proposition complete it.
- **Predictable answer rejected:** Luxury object centered above a button. Give the edition and its material history equal visual weight.
- **Direction:** luxury typography / gallery art direction.
- **Motion and interaction proposal:** Optional brief dissolve of the product view, triggered by intent; no automatic rotation.
- **Mobile:** Edition and image share one row; proposition and provenance follow.
- **Three industry translations:** Furniture: numbered edition; Winemaker: vintage release; Publisher: artist monograph.

## H24 — Made to Measure

Make the product's construction legible.

- **Mechanism:** A blunt stacked promise faces an exploded assembly diagram; a specification band sits at the joint between title and drawing. No generic capability cards.
- **Predictable answer rejected:** Brutalist all-caps slogan with random shapes. Tie every plane to a real configurable part.
- **Direction:** brutalist typography / billboard art direction.
- **Motion and interaction proposal:** Later part selection aligns one diagram plane with its specification. Static exploded state here.
- **Mobile:** Headline then assembly drawing; specifications become a plain semantic list.
- **Three industry translations:** Furniture: modular shelving; Building systems: panel assembly; Audio: modular hardware.

## H25 — Three Ways In

Orientation can be the first act of hospitality.

- **Mechanism:** Three typographic routes branch from one entry point across an open field. Destinations are the architecture, not decorative lines behind a headline.
- **Predictable answer rejected:** Three centered service cards. Use a clear route diagram with distinct destinations and one brand proposition.
- **Direction:** geometric typography / publication art direction.
- **Motion and interaction proposal:** Focus illuminates a route in a future prototype; never an auto-drawing background grid.
- **Mobile:** Branch becomes a vertical path with three full-width destinations.
- **Three industry translations:** Cultural campus: learn / see / meet; Travel group: coast / hills / city; Education: study / research / visit.

## H26 — Good Company

Personality comes from the invitation and type, not ornamental blobs.

- **Mechanism:** A compact conversational preface leads to a huge two-line promise. The final word is a visibly selected alternative; a broad reply line gives the visitor a next step.
- **Predictable answer rejected:** Bouncy headline, gradient shapes and pill cards. Build a language-led invitation with one explicit choice.
- **Direction:** playful typography / salon art direction.
- **Motion and interaction proposal:** Later choice changes one word and the action destination. No animated variable axes while reading.
- **Mobile:** Keep complete sentence order; selected word fits on its own line and alternatives become text links.
- **Three industry translations:** Repair collective: mend / reuse; Community kitchen: cook / share; Learning studio: make / discover.

## H27 — The Evidence Margin

The brand's point of view meets one piece of tangible evidence.

- **Mechanism:** A short editorial thesis occupies one continuous reading column; a full-height marginal strip pairs source, detail image and outcome. It is one argument, not a statistics grid.
- **Predictable answer rejected:** Testimonial quote card beside three metrics. Make evidence a margin bound to the main proposition.
- **Direction:** editorial typography / precision art direction.
- **Motion and interaction proposal:** No reveal required; optional source-note disclosure in a later prototype.
- **Mobile:** Thesis, detail image, then clearly attributed source and outcome; retain the reading order.
- **Three industry translations:** Conservation: material restoration; Legal practice: anonymized case insight; Engineering: tested construction detail.

## H28 — After Hours

The image holds the opening silence; the title arrives at the floor.

- **Mechanism:** An almost uncaptioned stage image dominates the upper field; a monumental condensed title anchors the bottom edge with the event information in a narrow vertical gutter.
- **Predictable answer rejected:** Centered cinematic slogan over a dark photo. Keep the scene open and place the title below its focal subject.
- **Direction:** poster typography / runway art direction.
- **Motion and interaction proposal:** A short exposure wipe on the image; title remains present from first paint.
- **Mobile:** Scene first, title beneath it, event gutter becomes a single line above the action.
- **Three industry translations:** Performance venue: evening series; Hospitality: supper club; Photography: nocturnal exhibition.

## Revisions made before presentation

- Rejected the early H25 row-list interpretation on desktop; replaced it with a staggered three-branch route field. Mobile retains a clear vertical path rather than tiny desktop branches.
- Replaced H20's repeated chair image with the existing human portrait study, giving the invitation a human subject. Removed the arbitrary arch-like framing from H20 and H23; the radius now serves image containment rather than becoming the concept.
- Corrected H21's inherited contain-fit: a panorama is its structural premise, so it uses cover. H27 likewise protects its evidence detail crop.
- Constrained H24's transformed planes inside their assembly field so they no longer collide with the masthead or specification strip.
- Varied action specimens with line, filled block and pill tendencies instead of keeping a single underline across the collection.
- Removed forced mobile minimum height that left an unearned blank tail on H19. Adjusted title scale/cascade so semantic type roles and intentional concept composition coexist.

These are refinements to static proposals. User selection can still reject any of the twelve; no passing test or pairwise matrix substitutes for that selection.
