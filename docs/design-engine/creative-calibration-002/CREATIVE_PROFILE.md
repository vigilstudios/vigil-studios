# Creative Calibration 002 — feedback profile

This is a working interpretation of the user's direct review of Creative Collection 001, not a permanent brand style guide. It should raise the quality bar without making future sites resemble one another.

## Approved qualities to pursue

- **Immersive, meaningful media:** H02's image occupies the full viewport and establishes the scene. H05's unusual interaction is welcome, with a stronger full-background media role.
- **Composed typography:** H02's font and low, contextual placement were praised. H01's editorial rail and H03's quiet minimalism also resonated. This supports expressive hierarchy and placement; it is not a mandate for one typeface.
- **Clear signature structure:** H01's side rail and N01's rounded floating island are memorable because they organize content, not because they add ornament.
- **Navigation independence:** N01–N04 were received positively. They should remain reusable systems that can pair with multiple heroes.
- **Soft geometry used intentionally:** Rounded edges, shaped media, and masks are welcome when they improve the media/content relationship.

## Avoided qualities to reject

- **Generic image-and-copy compositions:** H04 felt like an AI template. A second image and asymmetry alone did not create an original idea.
- **Generic modular business hero:** H06's capability grid failed the visual standard. Information blocks should not masquerade as a premium art direction.
- **Floating overlays without page logic:** H02's sharp square dark text box felt separate from the scene. Any content surface should be integrated with the viewport or follow the page's shape language.
- **Small, weak media roles:** H01's image felt undersized or insufficiently meaningful. A media element should carry narrative weight, not merely fill a vacant area.
- **Default web-library habits:** centered headline plus CTA, left/right split, arbitrary blobs, glass cards, startup type, safe symmetry, decorative animation, and minor variants are explicitly rejected in the Batch 002 brief.

The review did not name a general category of “overdesigned” elements. The sharp, detached H02 box is the concrete visual treatment the user asked to change; do not invent a blanket ban on layered or expressive design from that one example.

## Experimental opportunities

- A restrained **3D/spatial object** in a largely empty H03-like field, provided it communicates a subject, material, or choice and has a static fallback. This is a request for exploration, not blanket approval of decorative 3D.
- **Viewport-integrated surfaces** with a rounded edge or shaped boundary, especially where content overlays cinematic media.
- **Masking and shaped crops** that make image behavior part of the story. Avoid masks that are only a style flourish.
- **Full-background interactive states** so H05-like selection feels like a hero, with accessible non-hover control and calm transitions.
- More unusual relationships among type, media, edge, scroll, and visitor choice while preserving reusable content contracts.

## Motion and interaction reading

Direct evidence supports cinematic atmosphere (H02) and meaningful story selection (H05). The user did not praise motion for its own sake. Prioritize interaction that reveals content or changes viewpoint; keep transitions restrained and compatible with reduced motion. No preference for a specific animation duration or 3D technology has been established.
