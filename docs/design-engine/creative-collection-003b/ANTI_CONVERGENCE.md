## Closest pairs and decisions

- **NX01/NX02:** Meridian fixes the brand on its hinge and separates utilities and mobile priorities from its two wings. Datum keeps a single baseline and nonmodal fold. Retain both.
- **NX04/NX05:** Dock is an opaque bounded nonmodal fan. Viewfinder owns a media-safe band and a side modal that leaves a window onto the scene. Retain both studies; shortlist Viewfinder because existing Island already covers Dock's broad category.
- **NX03/NX08:** Dispatch continuously exposes edition/section hierarchy; Folio hands the viewport to navigation only on request. Prefer Dispatch for expansion because Contents already covers an editorial modal.
- **NX07/NX11:** Hall reveals groups together; Channel selects one department and moves focus to a child pane, with a Back stage on mobile. Taxonomy versus staged paths survives identical branding. Shortlist Hall for broad coverage.
- **NX09/NX10:** Margin reserves a side column. Threshold brackets an independent Hero with entrance and shelf. Both need composition ownership; prefer Margin first and review Threshold against actual page content.
- **NX10/NX12:** Threshold's shelf is a post-Hero landmark. Doors reveals a pre-Hero priority board. Their position, reading order and expansion differ even with identical rectangles and type.

Removed Pocket Dock's unhelpful left/right panel alignment and Atlas Hall's center-primary option during review: neither provided dependable structural value. Singleton declarations remain invariants rather than fake controls. No automatic creative approvals.
