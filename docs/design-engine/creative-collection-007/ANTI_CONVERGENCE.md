## Shared treatments deliberately retained

Accessible destination links, price semantics, contained imagery, five typography roles and the existing functional icon system are shared foundations. Most studies use rules and opaque inherited surfaces; the difference is in information ownership and rhythm, not decorative borders. Body-link affordances are intentionally consistent. None of the concepts depends on hover, a reveal direction or a particular animation duration to appear distinct. Motion is none across the round, so it earns no diversity credit. No Navigation structure is created; all compatibility suggestions refer to existing approved systems.

## Review outcome

Retain fourteen for human comparison, with six proposed first implementation proofs. No concept is counted solely because of typography, palette, ratio or number of columns. P03/P04 are deliberately different single-product merchandising strategies; P10/P11 are deliberately different gallery ownership models. P07/P14 share scroll mechanics and should share a future low-level sequence primitive while retaining separate content structures. P08's material proof and P09's provenance record must stay distinct from unsupported brand claims.

The first-context sequence uses ten profiles and never repeats a profile consecutively. Dense registers/worksheets, open object presentation, poster release, all-visible folio and native sequence create different reading rhythms. Three-context changes also alter product density, currency/price basis, options and photography instead of merely recoloring identical content.
