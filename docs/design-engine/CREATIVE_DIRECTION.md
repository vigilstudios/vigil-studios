## Originality must organize something

A strong concept gives the visitor a different way to discover, understand, inspect, compare, or move through meaningful information. Invent the relationship first: unequal editorial attention, a readable register, a shared scene and product legend, a detail inspection well, or a continuous visual essay. Distinct names, colors, corners, hover zooms or typefaces do not create distinct components.

Reject the predictable first answer when it fails to express the content. Generic SaaS hero/metric/card arrangements, decorative glass panels, default startup type, arbitrary blobs and cosmetic asymmetry do not meet this bar by default. A card or grid can still be appropriate when its structure solves an actual problem. M13's accepted gallery is evidence that the owner's standard is purposeful structure rather than a universal ban on grids.

Professional creativity should be memorable in its own right and reusable by replacing content, brand, type and compatible media. Growth remains bespoke client art direction, custom systems and choreography where the commission needs it. Scar's standalone Growth work remains excluded as source material. Professional is not a simplified Scar vocabulary.

## Typography supplies a distinct voice

Use independent display, heading, body, accent and mono roles. Explore materially different weights, widths, casing, tracking, italics, measures, numeral treatments and display/body contrast. A batch dominated by one elegant serif and quiet sans support has already failed calibration, even if palettes differ. Include left, centered, dense, open, type-led, photographic and technical relationships when the content warrants them.

Choose type and art direction independently of color. Load only selected, licensed families at the host boundary. Do not confuse broad profile compatibility with a promise that every client should use every profile. Strong mechanisms survive unrelated client voices; the chosen treatment should still be deliberate.

## Whitespace expresses rhythm

Whitespace allocates attention and connects parts. Unequal pauses can distinguish a campaign, a provenance receipt and a technical register. Empty space must earn its place through hierarchy, scene, object, interaction or reading pace. H15's static frame was not rescued by imagining a future animation. Equally, avoid filling every gap with cards or compressing every structure to the same density. Review neighboring sections as one reading experience, including double pauses and dense-to-dense transitions.

## Media carries narrative weight

Give images enough presence to communicate context, construction, texture or transformation. Whole objects, model views, campaigns, source imagery and macros serve different purposes. Close-ups should relate honestly to the overview; a gallery should not imply that unrelated products are alternate angles of one object. Captions, provenance and evidence belong to the content relationship.

Frames, masks, rounded edges, layered media and full backgrounds are legitimate when they reinforce meaning. The rejected arbitrary oval does not ban shaped media; the approved H09 specifically values purposeful rounding. Preserve complete objects where inspection matters, authored focal/crop behavior where cinematic context matters, and readable opaque text surfaces where imagery is variable. Missing media must preserve identity and access.

Generated Lab assets are illustrative. They do not establish manufacturing specifications, ingredients, customer outcomes or certifications. A client must supply accurate facts and appropriate photography before publication.

## Immersion includes visitor agency

The owner wants sections that invite engagement through compelling imagery, graphics, functional icons and purposeful animation. Selection should reveal meaningful content or a new viewpoint: compare actual states, inspect a material, explore a look, resolve supplied options, or follow an editorial sequence. Icons and diagrams need semantic work; M03 shows that decorative connectors are not sufficient.

A compelling resting frame comes first. Motion can enrich it, but cannot rescue a weak mechanism or hide evidence. Use the existing motion vocabulary only when implemented and verified. Do not make the same fade-up a house treatment. Preserve keyboard, touch, native links, named controls, focus and reduced-motion reading. No essential interaction depends on hover, tiny hotspots, dragging, or autoplay.

## Mobile is authored composition

Design the mobile relationship deliberately instead of stacking desktop columns. Maintain scene/legend/selected-record ownership, pair baseline/candidate criteria, wrap option groups with large targets, and distinguish a gallery contact sheet from a vertical essay. Native horizontal rails may reveal the next item and provide named controls; never intercept vertical scrolling. Test realistic copy, item limits, missing media and portrait/landscape mixes at actual device widths and narrow Lab artboards.

## Client direction remains independent

Future client work combines:

**Vigil creative principles + client Creative Package + client brand/assets + available engine mechanisms → client-specific art direction.**

The owner's preferences describe originality, intentionality and quality. The client defines voice, cultural context, content, audience, imagery and business priorities. Do not impose an editorial serif, streetwear photography, muted luxury palette, monochrome treatment or one of the current approved layouts on every business. Approved examples are evidence of durable principles, not a house style or a finite menu of acceptable looks. Keep exploring new relationships rather than reproducing favorites.
