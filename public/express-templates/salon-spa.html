Who we are

The studio

Ashcombe is a four-chair studio above the florist on Lantern Row. Appointments are booked with time either side, which means nobody is worked on in parallel and nobody sits waiting between steps. We keep notes on colour so you do not have to remember them.

  • One client at a time
  • Colour notes kept on file
  • Late Wednesday and Thursday
