Who we are

The shop

Quillon is a small homeware and stationery shop on Cobb Street. We buy from around sixty makers, most of them within a day's drive, and the shelves change through the year rather than staying the same. If something is not out, it is worth asking.

  • Free gift wrapping
  • 60+ small makers
  • Shelves change monthly
  • Cards and small gifts
