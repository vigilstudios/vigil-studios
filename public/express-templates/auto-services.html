Who we are

The shop

Halstead is an independent workshop with three bays on Foundry Lane. We work on most makes, petrol and diesel, and we put the car on the lift and show the owner the problem rather than describing it down the phone. Every job is quoted before it starts.

What we do

Services

What comes through the bay doors most weeks.

Diagnostics

Reading fault codes and tracing what actually caused them, with the findings explained before any part is ordered.

From $90

Brakes and suspension

Pads, discs, shocks and springs, including the knocking noises nobody can place over the phone.

From $180

Clutch and gearbox

Clutch replacement and gearbox work on manual and automatic cars.

Quoted per job

Tyres and alignment

Fitting, balancing and four-wheel alignment on the shop's own rig.

From $45 per tyre

Air conditioning

Regassing, leak tracing and compressor work for older systems.

From $120

State inspection

New York State safety and emissions inspection, booked by appointment.
