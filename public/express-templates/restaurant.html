Who we are

The place

Marlow & Fen is a thirty-four seat dining room on Weaver Street. The menu is short on purpose and changes with what the markets have, which means it is written each week rather than printed once a year. Bread is baked here in the morning and the kitchen is open to the room.

  • Menu written weekly
  • Bread baked in house
  • Thirty-four seats, one room
